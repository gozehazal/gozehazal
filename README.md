## Hi! I’m Hazal 💕 I am a climate and energy researcher interested in using data and quantitative methods to understand the energy transition.

# 😸 About Me:

I am an early-career climate, sustainability and energy researcher with a Master of Environmental Management from Duke University, specializing in Energy and Environmental Economics & Policy, and with a B.A. in International Relations from Bilkent University.

I am interested in climate mitigation, energy systems, environmental economics, and quantitative policy research. I enjoy using data to understand complex energy and climate challenges and translate findings into practical insights.

I work primarily with R and Python, with experience in statistical, energy modelling and econometric modelling, scenario analysis, data visualisation, and decision analysis. I am especially interested in finding ways to quantify and model qualitative indicators to better understand complex climate, energy, and policy questions. 

My recent research has included electricity reliability and energy-sector reform across African countries for the World Bank, transport decarbonization scenarios, and international energy and climate policy.

I am currently interested in expanding my work in climate mitigation pathways, energy-system modelling, decarbonization, and applied quantitative research.

While energy and sustainable development are my primary areas of technical expertise, my interest in sustainability extends beyond the energy transition. I am also interested in environmental governance and policy, life-cycle thinking, sustainable consumption, and how quantitative methods can be applied to broader sustainability challenges.
